Each trend gets its own deep-dive issue, in the order its evidence becomes available. Weekly issues
(`09` §3) continue in between. A deep dive is published only when the work in its "needs first" column is
done and checked; if the work slips, the issue slips — it is never written ahead of its data.

## Rules for every deep dive

- It leads with the trend, uses the three cities as evidence, and says which of them it appears in (`D-065`).
- Counts come from the Copenhagen frame (or, later, the London and Barcelona frames) with the denominator in
  the chart itself (`19` §3). Named-venue observations from the Phase 1 forty carry no count (`D-056`), except the fixed year-apart panel, counted as "N of the same 17 kitchens" with a watch-list label (`D-068`).
- Official statistics are national context, labelled as such, never presented as a measurement of our cities.
- Charts are generated by a script from the data files, never typed (`19` §4); the script lives in
  `charts/issue-NNN/`.
- A prediction is resolved in print when its date arrives, right or wrong, with the rule it was written with.

## What this calendar changes elsewhere
